What it does

Sessions that actually hold

Block any app or website for 5 minutes to 2 hours. Sessions keep running even if you force-quit NoFeed.

Profiles for how you work

Work, Study and Gym ready on day one. Build your own, each with its own block list. Switch in a tap.

Schedules that run themselves

Set recurring focus blocks. Your deep-work mornings stay protected without you remembering to start.

Progress worth returning for

Daily streaks, a productivity score from your real sessions, and a weekly breakdown of your focused hours.

Privacy

No accounts. No servers.

Your sessions, profiles, schedules and stats live on your iPhone and nowhere else. Apple's Screen Time framework hands NoFeed opaque tokens for the apps you block, so the app never learns which apps you use or how long you spend there.
