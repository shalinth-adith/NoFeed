NoFeed 2.3 — please test on a real iPhone. Blocking does nothing in the Simulator.

NEW IN 2.3
• Six languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese. Change the language in Settings › General › Language & Region, or per app in Settings › NoFeed › Language.
• After a session, say how it felt: Focused, Distracted or Interrupted. You can also answer from the session-end notification without opening the app.
• "What works for you" on Insights appears after 10 reflected sessions.
• Widgets: Home Screen widget with a Start button and live countdown, plus Lock Screen widgets.
• Strict mode: ending early now takes a 60-second hold.
• Settings › Send feedback opens a pre-filled email.

PLEASE CHECK
1. Start a session. Open a blocked app: the block screen appears. Tap "Back to focus".
2. Strict session: hold to end early. Letting go before 60 seconds cancels. A call or pulling down Control Center mid-hold also cancels.
3. Let a session finish while the app is in the background. Answer the notification with Focused, Distracted or Interrupted, then open the app: your answer is already selected.
4. Unlock window (Settings, off by default): turn it on, open a blocked app, and use the second button on the block screen. Everything unblocks, then blocks again when the window ends.
5. Add the Lock Screen widget during a session: the countdown runs. Tap the Home Screen widget's Start button: the session starts.
6. Switch to another language and look for cut-off text, English left behind, or wording that sounds wrong. Screenshots welcome.
7. A schedule shorter than 15 minutes still blocks.

Send anything odd with Settings › Send feedback, or by TestFlight screenshot feedback.
